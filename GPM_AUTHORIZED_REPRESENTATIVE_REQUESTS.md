# Authorized representative requests — G Putnam Music, LLC

Three letters. Same purpose: name one person who can act on GPM's accounts
without ever holding Gregory's own login.

**Before sending, fill in two things in each letter:**

- `[REPRESENTATIVE NAME]` — the person you nominate
- `[REPRESENTATIVE EMAIL]` — their email

Send each from **gputnam@gputnammusic.com**. That address is the account of
record; a request from anywhere else will be bounced back for verification.

**On disclosing a medical reason:** none of these letters mentions one, and
none needs to. You are entitled to appoint a representative for any reason,
and "as a matter of business continuity" is a complete answer. If you would
rather disclose, it can strengthen an accessibility accommodation request —
but that is your call to make, not something to include by default.
