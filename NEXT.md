## What your four rulings today changed

**"TWSTs & HOOKs are supreme!"** — both detectors were wrong and are fixed.

TWST fired on any line containing the word "but", which catches a line that
merely mentions it. A turn is a line that *bends*, in one of two shapes: it
opens against the line before it, or it bends mid-line with real words on
both sides of the pivot. **404 turns across 164 works.** 145 works do not
turn — that is the honest answer, not a gap.

HOOK had no idea what a repeated verse was. One song reported 20 hooks out of
28 lines: the supreme form came out the most common one. Now the first time
through is the hook, and the same song reports its **title line** first.
**5,647 hooks.**

**"By identical verses, are they EXACT repeats? If so, skip. If diff in any
way to human ear, keep alternatives."** — You caught me comparing spelling
and calling it hearing.

Folding a line to lowercase and calling two of them identical compares
letters. The same words sung at 0:45 and at 2:10 are two performances —
different breath, different placement, different music underneath. A text
file cannot tell them apart, so it no longer pretends to. Every later
rendition is kept and numbered: *"rendition 2 of 3 — same words, a different
take. Only the ear can say whether it is an exact repeat."* Each is its own
TUG candidate.

The only thing that still collapses is the same **bytes** — one Element
today, two titles over one sha256. That is a true exact repeat.

**"Mimic this behavior for ALL themes, all opportunities on ALL KUTs
platforms."** — It is law 16 now, not a note in one script.

The failure it stops looks like housekeeping: somebody writes a Set over
titles, the list gets tidier, a real alternative take stops existing, nothing
goes red, and the inventory just shrinks. Now it goes red. Proved by breaking
it on purpose — 681 groups failed, restoring made it green.

**"For MUSIC lovers, find all IN-PIX riffs."** — New, and it obeys the SSOT
law by separating nothing.

No stems, no filtering, nothing removed from the mix. It **selects** passages
of the master that have no vocal in them: every vocal span is already timed
and prosecuted, so the union of them is where the singing is, and what is
left is the band playing alone. A vocal's tail and the breath before an entry
belong to the vocal, so the margins are widened — and every pause a margin
closes is reported with its length, because a margin that quietly swallows a
turnaround is one nobody can audit.

Comin' True: an **11.55s intro riff** and a **4.45s outro**, both with real
music measured in them. No mid-song riff — the only pause closed was 0.27s, a
breath.
