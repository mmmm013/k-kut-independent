# Contributor & rights register

Started 2026-09-22.

**Why this file exists.** GD's stated ethos: *"ALL who gave their best talent to
my mission, I want them to receive some type reg royalty."* You cannot pay,
credit, or license from people you cannot name. Three times in one week a
contributor surfaced only by accident — a photographer found in EXIF, a
photographer named only in a forwarded email subject line, a credits list sent
once and lost. Each time the work was already published or about to be.

This register is the list. One row per person, per work. If a row is blank, the
work does not go out.

---

## Status key

- **CLEARED** — named, credited, terms in writing, on file.
- **NAMED** — we know who; terms not settled.
- **UNKNOWN** — we do not know who made this. Blocker.

## The instrument that already exists

GD has built this before. Two precedents in his own files, both worth reusing
rather than reinventing:

1. **Clayton Michael Gunn — Public Story & Privacy Approval Sheet** (2026-07-16):
   *"Before any website, press, interview, sponsor, collector, social-media, or
   exhibition material uses your personal story, I want you to control exactly
   what may be said and what must remain private."* The same instrument works
   for credits: **each contributor approves the exact wording of their own
   credit.**
2. **The Clayton representation deal**: 30% to GPM, only when a sale closes and
   is paid, GPM covers its own costs. A contribution-share that pays only out of
   money actually received, with the company absorbing its own overhead. That
   shape transfers directly to photographers and videographers.
