# GPMx / K-KUT — SWOT

**23 September 2026.** Every number below was measured today against the
repositories, not recalled. Where something could not be verified it says so.

---

## STRENGTHS

**The catalogue is real, owned, and clean.**
324 works. 324 unique valid ISRCs. 324 writer splits totalling 100%.
286 works where G Putnam Music publishes every writer share — no outside
publisher to clear, nobody to account to. Ten covers, every one marked
ineligible, none reachable by any Element.

**The audio is sound, and provably so.**
Boundaries prosecuted against aligned dual masters: 0.051s alignment lag,
0.176 vocal residual RMS, 20ms frames at 10ms hop. **Zero of 44 endpoints
land in vocal energy** — not one vocal is cut off anywhere in the song.
The Twinkle sits at correlation 1.0000 against the approved logo.

**Identity proves itself.**
Every delivered file is named for the first sixteen characters of its own
sha256. A file cannot pretend to be another file. Two independent routes —
the disk register and the buyer page — reach 61 sellable and agree exactly.

**Governance now blocks rather than reports.**
Eleven guards, all passing, all in prebuild. Until today they were reports
nobody ran, which is why six red deploys shipped unnoticed in one morning.
A banned name, an unexplained file in the delivery tree, or a changed hash
on a sellable Element now stops the build.

**Twenty years of work, and an unusual amount of it.**
87 works with Erik Nelson alone, his writer share equal to roughly 40 songs
owned outright. 29 distinct singers across those 87. The variety was a
deliberate discipline and it is measurable.

**The invention is genuinely novel.**
KUTs are not excerpts. The governed lineage, the boundary law, the tier
binding and the audio logo are a coherent system, documented and patented in
the repository. Nobody else is selling a sung line as a message.

---

## WEAKNESSES

**One song carries the entire business.**
46 boundary-truth files exist. **One has sections mapped.** One family has
been carried through to deliverable Elements. All 61 sellable items come
from *Comin' True*. The other 323 works have never been through 4PE steps 4
to 6.

**The main domain cannot reach the store.**
`k-kut.com` links to ten destinations. `/motivation`, which holds 61 of 61
Elements, is not one of them. A visitor to the flagship domain cannot find
the inventory. Raised three times today; still true.

**Capture grain is a thousand times coarser than the truth it is measured
against.**
50 of 83 Elements carry whole-second bounds. BLK boundaries are recorded to
the millisecond. 18 of 24 apparent boundary violations are rounding, not
structure — but the blur is real and it hides the ones that are structure.

**A section's own text field is empty.**
`lyrics_or_transcript` is null on all five BLKs while every one claims
STAGE. Because that field was never filled, a line-range pointer was
invented to stand in for it, and it indexes non-blank lines while every
consumer reads raw lines. That off-by-one produced wrong text on shipped
Elements.

**Synthesised text reached customers and nothing caught it.**
The MGS field named "inherited" had inherited nothing: one word of fourteen
matched the catalogue. 97 of 98 buyer intents were prose written about the
song rather than drawn from it. Three Elements sold "faith" where the song
sings "fate". All corrected today — but no guard existed, and none of it was
noticed by the system.

**One person is the single point of failure on everything.**
Twenty years of correspondence in one email account with no second copy. No
authorised representative on ASCAP, the MLC or HFA. A TBI that makes portals
hostile. If that account or that person is unavailable, nothing is reachable.

**Assumed controls that do not exist.**
No play limit, share limit or redemption cap exists anywhere in the code.
The purchase flow assumes them. Nothing enforces them.

## OPPORTUNITIES

**The multiplier is already proven.**
One song produced 101 Elements, 98 with finished audio. If that ratio holds
even loosely, 323 unprocessed works represent tens of thousands of sellable
Elements from masters already owned and already paid for. The pipeline that
made the first 98 is built, guarded and repeatable.

**1,287 candidates are already waiting.**
Across romance, wedding, anniversary, kupid and apology lanes. Zero finished.
These need prosecution, not creation.

**MetaGrab is the product nobody has built.**
"Help users find the right words to send" has a designed answer in the
interpretation doctrine: a phrase carries many readings, and the system
should offer them rather than collapse to one. That layer does not exist yet
in code. It is the difference between a clip store and a way of speaking.

**The Nelson catalogue is an asset and an obligation at once.**
Around a hundred co-written recordings, all registered, earning far below
what they should because few know they exist. Correct registration and
release serves Heather's family and GPM identically. Doing right here is not
a cost.

**Performance licensing is cheap and unblocks everything.**
An ASCAP digital service licence removes the entire public-playback question
across all five domains at once, for an annual minimum, and normally
backdates to first use.

## THREATS

**The catalogue dies every ten days and always has.**
Every source link is a presigned URL that expires. Nothing has ever been
mirrored. S3 answers "Request has expired" — the masters are there, the
signatures lapse. Until a mirror exists, the entire source library is
periodically unreachable.

**362 URLs point at storage that no longer exists.**
The `ii-delivery` bucket on project `vwlzubxshjjonabpeagd` returns
NoSuchBucket. All 104 HUGz seed previews depend on it, and 104 of 104 fail
their catalogue hash. That lane is dark and cannot be reopened from here.

**Thirty-eight works have unresolved publishing.**
The register says ONE STOP on 311 rows while naming ERSHKINSONGS as an
outside publisher on 38 of them. Both cannot be true. Only ASCAP's own
registration settles it, and that export has not arrived.

**Public playback without a licence.**
Audio plays publicly on three routes today. Owning the publishing does not
license the service; affiliation granted that right to ASCAP. Exposure is
small at this scale and backdating is normal — but it is real and it is
running now.

**Income pressure is the actual risk to quality.**
Nothing has been earned yet. Every shortcut that produced today's defects —
paraphrased tags, editorial intents, composed text, a preview page that
claimed to be a product — came from moving fast toward revenue. The pressure
is legitimate and it is also the mechanism by which the work gets damaged.

**Nothing is written down twice.**
The rights register contradicts itself. The worksheet lost an approval that
had been given. A boundary truth names five transition points and stores no
values. Institutional memory lives in one head and in files that disagree
with each other.

---

## What this adds up to

The assets are strong and the governance is now genuinely load-bearing. The
constraint is not quality, rights, tooling or law. **It is that one song out
of 324 has been through the process**, and the two things that would change
that are a fresh DISCO export and three hostnames in a settings field.

Everything built today exists so that when those masters land, nothing is
typed by hand and nothing goes wrong.
